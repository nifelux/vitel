Who We Are

Vitel Wireless is Nigeria's first Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) — a brand new blue network running on the 0712 prefix, officially launched on October 30, 2025.

Unlike traditional telcos, Vitel doesn't build its own towers or infrastructure. Instead, we roam and interconnect across Nigeria's four major networks, bringing you the best coverage at the best price.
